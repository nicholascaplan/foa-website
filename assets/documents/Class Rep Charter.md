**Class Rep Charter**

Being a class rep is a volunteer role and is intended as the bridge between class teachers and the parents, and also between the FOA and classes.

*Recommendation moving forward \-* 

*Setting up a rep team for the year, with at least one person from each class.*   
*Sharing responsibility within this team for the year as a whole to make the role less time intensive.*  
*This is of course only a suggestion and if classes prefer to work as a single entity that is up to the reps that year.*

**Defined Responsibilities of Reps:**

* Gathering contact information of parents.  
* Passing on messages from the class teachers.  
* Updates to classes for upcoming events and general reminders.  
* Requesting parents to assist with class trips.  
* Managing class kitties for the teacher gifts throughout the year.  
* Asking for parents to take point for Christmas Bazaar and Summer Fete.  
* Organising social events for classes/year groups

**Responsibilities outside the Rep remit:**

* Regularly summarising the full information from school newsletters, general email, etc. We all receive these so summarising simply duplicates information and adds to communications which can become confusing.   
* Taking point on the bazaar and fete. Although a class lead is required for these events it should not automatically be assumed that this will fall to the reps.  
* Managing complaints about school. If a parent has an issue or concern they need to raise it with the school directly. Advice always helpful of course but reps are not school staff and shouldn’t be treated as such.  
* Refereeing. We are all adults, and disagreements between people should be kept off the class groups, reps are not there to ref the group.
