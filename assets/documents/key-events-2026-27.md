### Event notes

- **Fireworks:** gates open at 16:30, the quiet display is at 17:00 and the main display is at 18:00. See the [Fireworks page](https://www.thefriendsofashley.org/events/fireworks-2026/) for tickets and practical details.
- **Christmas Fayre:** led by Rachel and Sophie.
- **Santa's Grotto:** run by parents and carers during the school day. Please keep it a surprise for the children.

## Meetings and volunteering

Meetings are informal and open to parents, carers and staff. Published opportunities include:

- **Co-Secretary:** a light, termly role covering agendas, minutes and paperwork; one person can do it or two can share.
- **Fireworks shadowing:** shadow Helen at Fireworks 2026 and learn how to lead the event in 2027, with no pressure.
- **Class reps:** share messages from The FOA with your class group.

See [Get Involved](https://www.thefriendsofashley.org/get-involved/) for details and [Meeting Minutes](https://www.thefriendsofashley.org/meeting-minutes/) for past meeting records.

## Happy Bags

Donate clothes to raise funds for the school. The published collections are Wednesday 13th January and Monday 26th April 2027, as listed above.

Bags are given to children in their classrooms. Clothes, shoes, accessories, bags, jewellery, hats, scarves and perfume are all welcome.

## Pre-loved Uniform

- New-logo items and coats: £3 per item.
- All other items: £1 per item.
- Card payments only.
- Donate uniform in the green bins by the School Office; no torn items.
- Reserve via the [Google Form](https://tinyurl.com/foapreloved), or email <foapreloveduniform@gmail.com> with sizes and quantities.
- Sales start at 15:25 in the school playground, weather permitting.

See the [Uniform page](https://www.thefriendsofashley.org/uniform/) for current instructions.
