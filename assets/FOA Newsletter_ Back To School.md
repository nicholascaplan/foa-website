| **Dear Parents and Carers,** Welcome back\! We hope you’ve all had a wonderful summer and are feeling refreshed, rested and ready for another brilliant year at Ashley. As the new school year gets underway, we thought it was the perfect time to say a big **hello from the Friends of Ashley (FOA) Committee**, give a little reminder about who we are and what we do, and, of course, get some important dates firmly into your diaries. The FOA is our school PTA, and it plays a huge part in bringing our community together, supporting the school and creating those extra special experiences and opportunities for our children. After such an incredible year last year \- including smashing our **£21,000 playground fundraising target** and raising an amazing **£25,730**, we’re feeling very excited about what we can achieve together this year. So, whether you’re a long-standing FOA supporter or you're completely new to the world of school fundraising and PTAs, read on\! 👇 **🤝 Friends of Ashley: Your School PTA** At Ashley, **every parent, carer and teacher is considered a member of the PTA**, also known as the *Friends of Ashley* or FOA. The FOA is supported and overseen by a **Committee of parent volunteers**, who are elected at our Annual General Meeting (AGM). The Committee helps to organise fundraising, coordinate events, oversee the FOA's finances and make decisions about how our funds can best support the school and our children. But here's the important bit… **You don't have to be on the Committee to be part of the FOA\!** 🎉 There are lots of ways to get involved, and volunteers are always needed. You might help out at an event, lend a hand with a pre-loved uniform, share an idea, volunteer for an hour or two at one of the events \- or even take charge of one of our seasonal events such as The Christmas Fayre or Summer fete. Remember, just coming along and showing your support really makes a difference.  **Call for Event Leads:** We are still looking for leads to run the Christmas Fayre, Quiz Night, Y3 & Y4 Disco, Summer Fete & Big Picnic. Please contact us for more info or come along to the AGM The FOA works best when it's a genuine team effort across the whole school community, and the more people who get involved, the more opportunities we can create for our children. If you have any ideas or questions, constructive feedback or simply keen to understand more… read on… 📧 **General FOA enquiries:[thefriendsofashley@gmail.com](mailto:thefriendsofashley@gmail.com)**  **📢 AGM: Come Along & Have Your Say\!** Our **Annual General Meeting will take place on Wednesday 16th September at 7:30pm at the school**.
