About The Friends of Ashley

All welcome. Every contribution matters.

The FOA is the parent teacher association for Ashley C of E Primary School.

What The FOA does

Community effort with visible impact.

Every parent, carer and teacher at Ashley is automatically a member. You do not need a committee title to take part.

01

Contribution

Families give time, ideas, donations and practical help in ways that suit them.

02

Collaboration

Parents, carers, teachers and volunteers work together on events and initiatives.

03

Community

The FOA creates welcoming opportunities to meet and support other Ashley families.

Last year's impact

£25,730 raised

The newsletter reports that this support is funding new KS1 playground equipment scheduled for installation over the October half term.

Accessibility

The FOA aims to make the website usable by as many people as possible.

Requires testing and formal approval before publication. This is a prototype statement, not a conformance claim.

How the prototype is designed

It uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-operable links and controls, visible focus styles, readable text, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Formal WCAG 2.2 AA testing, representative screen-reader testing, 200% zoom checks and external ticket-provider accessibility review have not yet been completed.
