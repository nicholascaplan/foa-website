# The Friends of Ashley Style Guide

## Organisation Name

- Use **The Friends of Ashley** as the organisation's full name in public-facing copy.
- Use **The FOA** as the shortened form after the full name has been introduced, including in titles, labels and compact copy.
- On first mention in a longer piece of copy, use **The Friends of Ashley (FOA)** when the abbreviation will be used later.
- Do not write **Friends of Ashley** without the initial **The** in public-facing copy.
- Do not write bare **FOA** in copy or titles; always include **The**.
- Keep the email address `thefriendsofashley@gmail.com` and other technical identifiers unchanged.

## Examples

- Correct: "The Friends of Ashley brings parents, carers and staff together."
- Correct: "The Friends of Ashley (FOA) committee welcomes volunteers."
- Correct: "Contact The FOA about volunteering."
- Incorrect: "Friends of Ashley brings parents, carers and staff together."
- Incorrect: "Contact FOA about volunteering."

## Scope

This guide applies to website content, metadata, navigation labels, structured data, review prototypes and project documentation. Supplied historical source material should follow this convention when it is edited or republished; source filenames, URLs, email addresses and code identifiers are not copy.
